# ☣ DEADFALL

## THE WORLD ENDED. YOUR CITY DIDN'T.

**DEADFALL** is an online multiplayer survival strategy game set in a world consumed by the undead.

Build your city. Raise your army. Gather resources. Defend your survivors. Explore the wasteland. Fight other survivors. Survive the hordes.

But remember:

**The dead aren't the only threat.**

---

## 🌍 ENTER THE DEAD WORLD

The collapse happened fast.

Cities fell.

Communications died.

Governments disappeared.

Millions became infected.

Now, scattered survivors are rebuilding civilization one settlement at a time.

You are one of them.

Your mission is simple:

### **SURVIVE. BUILD. CONQUER.**

---

# 🏙️ BUILD YOUR CITY

Start with a small survivor settlement and turn it into an impenetrable stronghold.

Construct and upgrade:

* 🏛️ Town Hall
* 🌾 Farms
* 🪵 Lumber Yards
* ⚔️ Barracks
* 🔧 Workshops
* 📡 Watchtowers
* 🏥 Hospitals
* 🔫 Armories

Every building matters.

Every upgrade costs resources.

Every decision affects your survival.

---

# ⚔️ BUILD YOUR ARMY

You aren't surviving alone.

Train survivors and turn them into soldiers.

Deploy:

### 🔫 Riflemen

Reliable ranged fighters capable of defending your city and attacking enemy settlements.

### 🛡️ Guards

Heavy defenders designed to protect your strongest positions.

### 👁️ Scouts

Fast reconnaissance units capable of exploring dangerous territory and gathering intelligence.

Your army is your shield.

Your army is also your weapon.

---

# 🧟 THE HORDE IS COMING

The wasteland isn't safe.

Zombie hordes can emerge without warning and threaten entire regions.

Prepare your defenses.

Stock your resources.

Deploy your army.

And hope your walls hold.

Because when the Horde arrives...

**everyone is a target.**

Players fight.

And the world continues evolving even when you're offline.

### Your city is part of the world.
